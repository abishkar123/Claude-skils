# Example: Weekend Plan (Manual Mode)

> **User request:** "Plan my weekend. Exhausted (2/5). Need groceries, laundry,
> renew car insurance, want to study system design, dinner with friends
> Saturday 7pm, and I should prep for Monday's presentation."

## Weekend Plan: Jun 20–21

**Objective:** Recover energy while clearing errands and doing the minimum useful Monday prep — without turning Sunday into a workday.

**Inputs used:** Manual only: energy 2/5, 3 errands, 1 study wish, 1 fixed social event, Monday presentation.

**Assumptions:** Waking hours ~9am–11pm. Presentation already exists and needs rehearsal, not creation (if it doesn't exist yet, tell me — that changes everything).

### Prioritized Actions
**Must-do**
- [ ] Errand batch Sat 10:30–12:30: groceries + start laundry + insurance renewal online (~2h)
- [ ] Monday prep Sun 5:00–5:30pm: one rehearsal run + top-3 for Monday
**Should-do**
- [ ] One 60-min system-design read, Sun ~11am — only if you feel like it
**Optional**
- Nothing else. That's deliberate.

### Capacity Check
At energy 2/5 the rest floor rises to **≥50% unstructured time**. Scheduled: ~4h of 28 waking hours (14%). Plenty of slack — correct for an exhausted weekend.

| Block | Sat | Sun |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Morning | sleep in, slow start | unstructured (optional 60-min study) |
| Afternoon | errand batch 10:30–12:30, then free | free |
| Evening | dinner with friends 7pm | Monday prep 5–5:30, then free |

### Risks & Overload Warnings
- ⚠ The study block is the first thing to drop. At 2/5 energy, skipping it is the plan working, not failing.

### Tradeoffs
Study time cut from "afternoon of studying" to one optional hour — recovery this weekend buys back the focus next week. **Not this weekend:** deep study, side projects, any extra admin.

### Success Criteria
Errands done by Sat 1pm · rehearsal happened Sunday · at least one fully unscheduled half-day · you end Sunday less tired than you started Saturday.
**Minimum viable weekend:** insurance renewed + Monday rehearsal.

**Review checkpoint:** 2 lines at Sunday Monday-prep: "rested? ready?"

**Manual fallback:** This *is* the manual workflow — no tools involved.
