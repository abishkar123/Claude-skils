# Review Templates

## Daily Review

Use at end of day or start of next day.

Questions:
- What got completed?
- What mattered most?
- What slipped and why?
- What interrupted focus?
- What should be carried forward, delegated, deferred, or deleted?
- What is the first priority tomorrow?

Scores 1-5:
- Priority clarity
- Focus
- Energy management
- Follow-through
- Recovery

## Weekly Review

Questions:
- What were the top wins?
- Which priorities advanced?
- What commitments were missed or renegotiated?
- What consumed more capacity than expected?
- What should be repeated?
- What should stop?
- What evidence, lessons, or artifacts should be saved?

Outputs:
- Wins
- Lessons
- Unfinished commitments
- Capacity insight
- Next-week priorities
- Evidence log updates

## Monthly Review

Questions:
- What changed this month?
- Which goals moved forward?
- Which goals no longer matter or need reframing?
- What habits improved or degraded?
- What professional evidence was created?
- What skill progress is visible?
- What should be protected next month?

Scores 1-5:
- Goal progress
- Execution reliability
- Skill growth
- Career evidence
- Health and recovery
- Relationships
- Admin/financial order

Output:
- Month summary
- Progress by goal
- Evidence log
- Habit review
- Skill review
- Keep/change/stop decisions
- Next month priorities

## Quarterly Reset

Use for broader direction setting.

Procedure:
1. Review the last quarter by outcomes, not busyness.
2. Identify themes: energy, work, learning, relationships, health, money, environment.
3. Retire stale goals.
4. Choose 1-3 quarterly outcomes.
5. Define leading indicators.
6. Convert outcomes into monthly milestones.
7. Define review dates and failure signals.

## Goal Refinement

Convert vague goals into:
- Outcome
- Why it matters
- Current state
- Target state
- Constraints
- Leading actions
- Evidence of progress
- Review cadence
- Stop or revise condition
